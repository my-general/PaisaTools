# Insurance Planning: Protect Your Family's Future

Insurance isn't an investment - it's protection against financial emergencies. Having the right insurance coverage is crucial for financial stability.

## Types of Insurance You Need

### 1. **Health Insurance**
* **Why:** Medical inflation is 15-20% annually
* **Coverage:** Minimum ₹5-10 lakhs per family member
* **Top-up:** Super top-up for higher coverage at lower cost

### 2. **Term Life Insurance**
* **Why:** Income replacement for your family
* **Coverage:** 10-15 times annual income
* **Tenure:** Until retirement or financial independence

### 3. **Personal Accident Insurance**
* **Why:** Covers disability and accidental death
* **Coverage:** Similar to life insurance amount
* **Benefits:** Additional to life insurance

### 4. **Critical Illness Insurance**
* **Why:** Covers specific life-threatening diseases
* **Coverage:** Lump sum payment on diagnosis
* **Diseases:** Cancer, heart attack, stroke, etc.

## Insurance vs Investment

### Pure Insurance Products
* **Term Life Insurance**
* **Health Insurance**
* **Low cost, high coverage**
* **Buy these first**

### Investment-Linked Products
* **ULIPs (Unit Linked Insurance Plans)**
* **Endowment Plans**
* **Money-back Policies**
* **Higher cost, lower returns**
* **Avoid mixing insurance with investment**
