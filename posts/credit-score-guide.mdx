# Credit Score Guide: The Key to Better Loan Terms

Your credit score is your financial report card. A good score can save you lakhs in interest over your lifetime, while a poor score can make borrowing expensive or impossible.

## What is a Credit Score?

A **credit score** is a 3-digit number between 300-900 that represents your creditworthiness. It's calculated based on your credit history and repayment behavior.

### Score Ranges and Meaning
* **750-900:** Excellent (Best loan terms)
* **700-749:** Good (Favorable terms)
* **650-699:** Fair (Higher interest rates)
* **600-649:** Poor (Limited approval)
* **300-599:** Very Poor (Difficult to get loans)

## Factors Affecting Your Credit Score

### 1. **Payment History (35%)**
* Timely EMI and credit card payments
* Defaults and delays negatively impact

### 2. **Credit Utilization (30%)**
* Percentage of available credit used
* Keep below 30% of your credit limit

### 3. **Credit History Length (15%)**
* Age of your oldest credit account
* Longer history is better

### 4. **Credit Mix (10%)**
* Healthy combination of different credit types
* Secured + unsecured loans

### 5. **New Credit (10%)**
* Number of recent credit inquiries
* Too many applications hurt your score

## How to Improve Your Credit Score

### Immediate Actions (1-3 months)
* Pay all pending dues immediately
* Reduce credit card utilization below 30%
* Avoid new credit applications

### Medium-term Strategies (3-6 months)
* Set up payment reminders
* Keep old credit cards active
* Maintain a healthy credit mix

### Long-term Habits (6+ months)
* Always pay bills on time
* Monitor your credit report regularly
* Maintain low credit utilization

## Common Credit Score Myths

### ❌ Myth: Checking score reduces it
**Truth:** Checking your own score doesn't affect it

### ❌ Myth: No loans = Good score
**Truth:** No credit history = No score

### ❌ Myth: Debit card usage helps
**Truth:** Only credit products affect score

### ❌ Myth: Closing old cards improves score
**Truth:** Can actually reduce your score

## Getting Your Free Credit Report

In India, you can get one free credit report per year from each bureau:
* CIBIL
* Experian
* Equifax
* CRIF High Mark

## Maintaining an Excellent Score

### Do's:
✅ Pay EMIs and credit cards on time
✅ Keep credit utilization low
✅ Review credit report annually
✅ Maintain healthy credit mix

### Don'ts:
❌ Max out credit cards
❌ Apply for multiple loans simultaneously
❌ Close oldest credit cards
❌ Guarantee loans for unreliable people

    

**Pro Tip:** A good credit score can save you ₹5-10 lakhs on a home loan through lower interest rates. It's worth the effort!
